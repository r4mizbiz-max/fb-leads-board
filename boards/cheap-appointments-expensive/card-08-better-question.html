Change Your First Question

Stop asking "how much per appointment?" Ask: what happens after I buy it?

Price tells you almost nothing. These are the questions that actually predict whether an appointment becomes a bathroom:

1How is the homeowner generated?
2What exactly did they respond to?
3What questions were they asked?
4What makes them qualified?
5Are the appointments exclusive?
6Are they confirmed before I get them?
7What happens with no-shows?
8What's the actual show rate?
9And the one that matters most: what % of these appointments actually become revenue?
💡 That last number is the one paying your bills — not the price on the invoice.
