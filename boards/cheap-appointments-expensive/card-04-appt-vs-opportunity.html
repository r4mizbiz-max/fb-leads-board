The Biggest Distinction

A booked calendar slot is not a sales opportunity.

These are two completely different products — but both get sold to you as "an appointment." Someone can agree to a time and still have nothing behind it.

❌ Appointment A · just a slot
Yeah, I guess someone can come by.
✕ No urgency
✕ No realistic project
✕ No decision-maker present
✕ No idea what remodeling costs
✕ Barely remembers your company
✅ Appointment B · a real opportunity
We're actively looking to replace our shower.
✓ Real urgency & timeline
✓ A defined, funded project
✓ Both decision-makers there
✓ Understands the investment
✓ Expecting your call
💡 Both go on the calendar. Only one can turn into a bathroom. Stop obsessing over the sticker price of a slot.
